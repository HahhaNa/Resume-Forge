XCharter, by Michael Sharpe — an extension of Bitstream Charter.
Subset here to the Latin-1 range plus the punctuation the resume uses; the
originals ship with TeX Live under fonts/opentype/public/xcharter.

The preview embeds these so the browser measures text with the same metrics
pdflatex does through \usepackage{charter} — same digitization, same advance
widths, so a line that wraps in one wraps in the other.
